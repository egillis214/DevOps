# Enterprise Implementation Case Studies

## Case Study 1: Global CMDB Remediation & Asset Alignment
* **Situation**: A Fortune 500 enterprise suffered from a bloated, un-attenuated CMDB containing 40% duplicate Configuration Items (CIs). This led to failed IT software compliance audits and inaccurate ITSM incident routing.
* **Task**: Act as Executive Sponsor and Lead Director to overhaul the CMDB architecture, enforce CSDM frameworks, and align physical assets with technical discovery.
* **Action**: 
  * Deployed ServiceNow Service Graph Connectors to consolidate disparate infrastructure data pools.
  * Established automated CI lifecycle management and Data Certification schedules for data owners.
  * Reconciled Software Asset Management (SAM) entitlements directly against discoverable active software instances.
* **Result**: Achieved **98% CMDB data health score** within 9 months, reclaimed **$2.4M in dormant software licenses**, and eliminated audit vulnerabilities.

## Case Study 2: ITSM Modernization and GenAI Integration
* **Situation**: Legacy IT service management processes resulted in high ticket bounce rates and an average Mean Time to Resolution (MTTR) exceeding 18 hours.
* **Task**: Redesign enterprise-wide Incident, Problem, and Change Management workflows leveraging modern Now Platform capabilities.
* **Action**:
  * Directed the migration of legacy workflows to Flow Designer.
  * Implemented Now Assist GenAI capabilities for conversational self-service and automated ticket categorization.
  * Enforced mandatory CMDB CI binding on P1/P2 incidents to enable automated root-cause impact analysis.
* **Result**: Slashed ticket bounce rates by **42%**, reduced MTTR from 18 hours down to **4.2 hours**, and boosted user CSAT scores to an all-time high of **94%**.
